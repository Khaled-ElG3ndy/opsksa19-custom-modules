POS Custom Receipt

Customize your Point of Sale receipts effortlessly, tailoring the layout, content, and branding to match your business needs.

Fully customizable receipts

The client and the VAT breakdown are printed on the receipt screen, on the printed ticket and on every reprint.

No configuration required

Install the module and both additions appear. There is nothing to set up, per shop or otherwise.

Arabic and English

Every label is translated, and the receipt mirrors with the interface for a right-to-left session.

Client Name on POS Receipt

The customer set on the order is printed as a labelled line, directly under Served by.

Served by: Mitchell Admin Client: Billy Fox 1 Corner Desk Left Sit 97.75

A customer under a company prints as Client: Deco Addict, Billy Fox. An order with no customer prints no client line.

VAT Summary on POS Receipt

A table at the foot of the receipt, one line per VAT rate on the order.

CHANGE 0.00 VAT% VAT ExVAT Total 15 % 12.75 85.00 97.75

Rows are per tax, so a 15% item and a zero-rated item are told apart even when the chart of accounts files them under one tax group.

اسم العميل على الإيصال

يُطبع العميل المحدد في الطلب في سطر مُعنون، أسفل سطر تم تقديم الخدمة بواسطة مباشرة.

تم تقديم الخدمة بواسطة: متشل أدمن العميل: Billy Fox

العميل التابع لشركة يُطبع باسم الشركة أولاً. والطلب الذي بلا عميل لا يُطبع له سطر عميل.
